Who We Are

From a single station to a Kingdom-wide network

Founded in 1995, Petrogen began as a single fuel station and has grown into a mature, multi-service enterprise. Since our 2018 transformation, we've combined fuel distribution, retail, and roadside care into one dependable network — always close to you.
