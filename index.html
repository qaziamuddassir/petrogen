Our Story

From a single station to a Kingdom-wide network

Founded in 1995, Petrogen began as a single fuel station and has grown into a mature, multi-service enterprise combining fuel distribution, retail, and roadside care into one dependable network.

  • ISO-certified operations across the network
  • 24/7 roadside and technical support
  • Fast, reliable service at every stop
