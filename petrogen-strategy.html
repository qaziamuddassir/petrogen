Our Long-Term Direction

Growing responsibly, station by station

Petrogen's strategy rests on four pillars: expanding our network into new cities, investing in cleaner energy options like EV charging, strengthening the certified standards behind every station, and building long-term partnerships with franchisees and vendors — all in support of the Kingdom's broader energy and sustainability goals.

Four Strategic Pillars

How we plan to grow

Network Expansion

Reaching new cities and highways through franchising and direct development.

Cleaner Energy

Rolling out EV charging and efficient operations across stations.

Certified Standards

Maintaining ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 across every location.

Lasting Partnerships

Investing in franchisees and vendors as long-term stakeholders.
